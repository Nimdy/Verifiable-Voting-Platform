# ADR-0011: Selene-style verifiable trackers add transparent individual verifiability + coercion-MITIGATION (never coercion-resistance), reusing the existing mixnet + threshold primitives

**Status:** Accepted (reduced-but-honest variant shipped; the operative coercion guarantee is deferred to M3+)

## Context
The single most-cited objection to remote/digital E2E-V is coercion: a voter watched, paid, or pressured cannot be protected by a published transcript. The kernel already says so plainly (SCOPE/THREAT_MODEL) and the strong answer — JCJ/Civitas coercion-*resistance* via fake credentials — is large, assumption-heavy, and milestone work (M6). A practical middle path is **Selene** (Ryan–Rønne–Iovino, ESORICS 2016): each voter gets a private **tracking number**; after the tally, `(tracker, vote)` rows are posted in cleartext; each voter privately recovers her tracker and reads her own vote — *transparent individual verifiability with no per-voter ZK to interpret* — and, under coercion, can fabricate a deniable opening that points her notification at a different, palatable row. This is coercion-**mitigation**, strictly weaker than coercion-resistance, and it composes cleanly with primitives the kernel already has (the Sako–Kilian verifiable mixnet, k-of-n threshold decryption, the NUMS Pedersen generator `H`, and the generalized-Schnorr/Chaum–Pedersen proof templates).

A design panel (three cryptographers) plus a coercion-resistance skeptic produced two load-bearing rulings: **(1)** equivocation MUST be on the *ephemeral opening*, never the trapdoor key — a fake key `tk′` is publicly falsified by the posted dual-key `pk = x·G`; only `a′ = x⁻¹·(β − T′)` (key unchanged) is sound; **(2)** the tracker MUST be a group **point** (the kernel's `discreteLog` is O(max) brute force — an integer tracker would hang), recovered by point-equality.

## Decision
Ship the **reduced-but-honest** Selene variant (`reference/src/selene.ts`, + `proveTrackerConsistency`/`verifyTrackerConsistency` in `proofs.ts`), composed only from existing primitives with **no new cryptographic assumption** (DDH + Fiat–Shamir/ROM):

- **Tracker** `T` = a group point. **Encrypted tracker** `ET = pointEncrypt(PK, T, ρ) = (ρ·G, T + ρ·PK)` under the trustees' joint key. **Perfectly-hiding commitment** `Com = T + d·H` (kernel NUMS `H`), bound to `ET` by `proveTrackerConsistency` — a two-scalar generalized Schnorr where the tracker *cancels* in `ET.b − Com = ρ·PK − d·H`, so no point-witness is needed. **Notification** `(a, β) = (r·G, T + r·pk)` toward the voter's dual key `pk = x·G`.
- **Tally:** each ballot becomes a width-(1+K) item `[ET, enc₀…enc_{K-1}]`; `shuffleProve`/`verifyShuffle` (Sako–Kilian, 2⁻¹²⁸) permute+re-encrypt them; k-of-n threshold decryption (with Chaum–Pedersen proofs) opens every column — the tracker as a **point**, the votes as bits — and posts the cleartext `(tracker, vote)` rows. A verifier re-derives `L0` from the board, re-checks the shuffle, all decryption proofs, the vote-validity and tracker-consistency proofs, recomputes the rows, and enforces **tracker-point collision-freeness**.
- **Retrieval** `T = β − x·a` (point lookup, no ZK). **Equivocation** `a′ = x⁻¹·(β − T′)` opens the *same* notification to any posted row with `x` (and `pk = x·G`) unchanged. The notification and its designated-verifier correctness proof travel OFF the board (untappable channel), never as a universal recheckable NIZK — a public one would let a coercer recheck the opening and kill deniability.

Held to the project bars: a second independent **Python/libsodium** verifier (`verify_selene`), a cross-language divergence battery (honest control + tamper classes — flipped vote, swapped/forged tracker commitment & proof, corrupted decryption share, tampered shuffle, forged signature, duplicate tracker, non-canonical scalar, **duplicated eligible roll, and float-encoded counts**) on which the TS and Python verdicts are **equal in every case** (a round-18 review found and fixed two divergences here — a missing TS duplicate-roll check and a TS/Python `2` vs `2.0` numeric-encoding gap latent across all transcript kinds; see CRYPTO_REVIEW round 18), CI re-verification of `out/selene.json` in both languages, and a self-test battery (`selftest.ts` §25: election/retrieval/equivocation/tamper/consistency-unit/never-throws).

### The strongest claim that is true
Reusing the kernel's primitives with no new assumption, this layer adds **(1) transparent individual verifiability** — after the shuffled, threshold-decrypted `(tracker, vote)` rows are posted, voter *i* recovers her tracker point `T = β − x·a` with only her trapdoor key and reads her own vote among the public rows, with no per-voter ZK; and **(2) coercion-MITIGATION / receipt-freeness for that tracker** — under coercion she fabricates a self-consistent ephemeral opening `a′ = x⁻¹·(β − T′)` to any posted row while her key and published `pk = x·G` are never repudiated, so the fake is indistinguishable to a coercer lacking `x` and the untappable channel. The pairing is a universally-verifiable permutation+re-encryption (2⁻¹²⁸) + threshold decryption of the committed board. Integrity is classical (DDH/ROM).

### MUST-NOT-CLAIM
1. **NOT coercion-resistance** (JCJ/Civitas). Mitigation only; no fake credentials.
2. **NOT effective without an untappable channel** for the trapdoor key `x` and the notification.
3. **NOT effective without an eager, coercion-free retrieval window** — a coercer who returns after results post and demands the opening defeats it unless the voter pre-committed a fake story.
4. **NO mitigation in this PoC posture / under issuer-generated keys.** The reference generates `x` and assigns trackers in-process (like the simulated DKG). So the property is cryptographically **demonstrated** but **not operative**: it requires VOTER-CONTRIBUTED `x` and DISTRIBUTED tracker assignment (no single party learns voter↔tracker) — milestone M3+.
5. **NO defense against the forced-instruction ("Italian") attack.** The full `(tracker, vote)` multiset is public; a coercer who demands a specific rare ballot verifies it directly from the board, no tracker needed. This is the dominant residual attack.
6. **NOT perfectly hiding under the dual key** — the `(s·G, s·pk + T)` form is computational (CDH); only the Pedersen `T + d·H` commitment is perfectly hiding.
7. **Do NOT equivocate by faking the key** — `tk′·G ≠ pk` is publicly falsifiable; equivocation is on the ephemeral opening only.
8. **NO universal/transferable dispute resolution** — a voter who sees the wrong vote has only a non-transferable complaint (a known Selene limit).
9. **NO turnout/participation privacy** — `credentialPub`/`pk` are public; forced-abstention still works.
10. **NO everlasting privacy / ballot secrecy of the posted rows** — they are cleartext under computational DDH; weakly de-anonymizing for unique votes in small electorates.
11. **NO privacy against colluding trustees + whoever knows identity↔`pk`.**
12. **NOT post-quantum** — a curve break forges trackers and de-anonymizes.
13. **NOT audited, NOT constant-time.** Pre-audit.

## Consequences
GOOD: gives the kernel a real, sound, cross-verified answer to "how does a voter check her own vote?" and the *mechanism* for receipt-freeness, composed from already-reviewed primitives with no new assumption; the honest scope is enforced in code, demo, this ADR, and the verifiers. BAD/COSTS: it is mitigation, not resistance, and in the current posture the headline coercion property is demonstrated-not-operative (needs M3+ voter keys + M4 distributed assignment); the Italian attack and the untappable-channel/eager-retrieval assumptions are real and load-bearing; the posted rows are only computationally private. These are accepted and stated plainly — an honestly-scoped Selene is genuinely useful and a stepping stone, whereas claiming coercion-resistance would be exactly the over-claim this project forbids.

## Alternatives considered
(1) **JCJ/Civitas coercion-resistance now** — REJECTED for this increment: large, assumption-heavy (untappable registration), milestone M6; Selene is the tractable, honestly-weaker first step. (2) **Faithful fully-distributed Selene now** (voter-contributed keys + distributed tracker assignment) — DEFERRED: the distributed no-single-party assignment (M4) is the hardest, most error-prone soundness surface and is not soundly shippable yet; ship the verifiable core + the mechanism, banner the gap. (3) **Equivocate the trapdoor key** — REJECTED: publicly falsifiable via `pk = x·G`. (4) **Integer trackers via `discreteLog`** — REJECTED: O(max) brute force hangs; trackers are points matched by equality. (5) **A public, universally-recheckable notification NIZK** — REJECTED: it lets a coercer recheck the opening and destroys deniability; the notification proof is designated-verifier / off-board.
