# ADR-0008: Receipt-freeness: never give a voter a transferable proof of how they voted

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The original concept's Step 17 -- 'the voter is provided confirmation and can see their vote listed within the Voting Blockchain' -- and Step 18's handing the printed paper ballot to the voter both create a TRANSFERABLE RECEIPT: cryptographic or physical evidence the voter can show a third party to prove how they voted. Such a receipt is precisely the instrument of vote-buying and coercion (sell your vote, prove you complied; or a coercer demands proof). This directly undermines the project mission of removing footholds for cheating and the 'reveal nothing' half of 'verify everything, reveal nothing.' The tension is that E2E-V REQUIRES the voter to verify their ballot was recorded (recorded-as-cast) -- so the design must let a voter check inclusion WITHOUT producing anything that proves the vote's CONTENT to anyone else. Note the reference transcript already publishes only encrypted ballots and aggregate decryptions (src/election.ts Transcript), never per-voter plaintext, which is the foundation receipt-freeness builds on.

## Decision
No voter ever receives a transferable proof of how they voted. Concretely: (a) only ENCRYPTED ballots, validity proofs, nullifiers, and the signed Merkle root are published; per-voter plaintext is never published and only the AGGREGATE is decrypted after close (homomorphic tally + threshold decryption). (b) Recorded-as-cast is delivered by a PRIVACY-PRESERVING tracking commitment plus a Merkle inclusion proof against signed roots -- it proves the voter's ciphertext is included but reveals nothing about the choice. (c) Cast-as-intended uses Benaloh cast-or-challenge: a challenged ballot is spoiled and audited (revealing its randomness for verification) and is NOT counted, while a cast ballot's randomness is never revealed -- so a voter cannot prove the content of the ballot they actually cast. (d) The 'see your vote on the chain' step from the original concept is explicitly removed. (e) In the polling-place profile the paper ballot of record is retained under custody behind glass, NOT handed to the voter (consistent with ADR-0004), because handing back reconciled paper is itself a coercion receipt. (f) Stronger coercion-resistance is available via OPTIONAL tiers selected in the manifest: Selene (deferred, fakeable trackers that let a coerced voter present a convincing false tracker), MACI/Semaphore (zk anti-collusion / key-change to invalidate a bribe), or full JCJ/Civitas (fake credentials). The platform's stated scope is low-coercion, recoverable contests; the optional tiers extend resistance where needed.

## Consequences
GOOD: vote-buying and coercion become impossible-by-construction in the strong tiers and structurally discouraged in the default tier; verifiability (inclusion + tally) is preserved without sacrificing secrecy, satisfying 'verify everything, reveal nothing'; removes the single most dangerous instinct of the original concept; the published transcript leaks nothing about individual choices. BAD/COSTS: receipt-freeness complicates the UX of 'prove to ME that MY vote counted' -- voters get a privacy-preserving inclusion proof rather than a plaintext receipt, which is less intuitive and requires user education; the default Belenios/homomorphic tier provides receipt-freeness but NOT full coercion-resistance against an adversary who watches the voter cast in real time (an over-the-shoulder coercer) -- true coercion-resistance needs the optional Selene/MACI/JCJ tiers, which carry real costs (Selene: trust in tracker distribution; MACI: a coordinator and zk circuits; JCJ: quadratic tally cost + untappable-registration assumption); this is why the honest scope is explicitly low-coercion/recoverable, documented in SCOPE.md. ACCEPTED: the residual coercion exposure of the default tier is the reason binding/high-coercion elections are out of scope, not a defect to paper over.

## Alternatives considered
(1) Show the voter their decrypted vote / 'see your vote on the chain' (original) -- rejected: a perfect vote-buying receipt. (2) Give the voter the ballot's encryption randomness as a receipt -- rejected: randomness + published ciphertext proves the plaintext to anyone, i.e. a receipt. (3) Plaintext tracking numbers tied to choices -- rejected: re-linkable receipt. (4) Selene-style fakeable trackers as the DEFAULT -- deferred to an optional tier: adds tracker-distribution trust and UX complexity not warranted for the low-coercion default scope. (5) Full JCJ/Civitas coercion-resistance as default -- rejected as default: quadratic tally cost and an untappable-registration assumption make it too heavy for clubs/DAOs; offered as the strongest optional tier. The chosen baseline (encrypted-only publication + privacy-preserving inclusion proof + Benaloh + no paper handed back) gives receipt-freeness cheaply, with stronger coercion-resistance available on demand.
